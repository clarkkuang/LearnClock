📖 How to Play

  • Drag the hands: grab the long gold minute hand or the short jade hour hand. When the minute hand goes all the way round, the hour hand moves on by 1 hour — just like a real clock.
  • Digital clock: under the dial you get the 12-hour time, the 24-hour time and the time in words.
  • Let time pass: choose “hours + minutes” or “minutes only”, then press ▶ and watch the hands travel.
  • Remember: 1 hour = 60 minutes · half an hour = 30 minutes · a quarter of an hour = 15 minutes.
  • Rewards: every correct answer earns a ⭐, and every 5 stars hatches a baby dragon 🐲.
